About Deora 29

Natural care, royal you

Deora 29 makes hair and skin care inspired by herbs: from the Hema Kesh serum and herbal hair oil to shampoo, black herb hair colour and face clean powder. Our promise is simple: real herbs, real care, and products you can trust.

How to use Hema Kesh Serum

Take a few drops on your palm, warm between your hands and massage into scalp and hair. Leave in or rinse as directed on the bottle.

How do I use the hair oil?

Massage into scalp and hair for 5–10 minutes, leave for at least an hour or overnight, 3–4 times a week.

How do I use the Black Herb colour?

Mix 100 g powder with 3–4 parts lukewarm water, apply evenly, wash after 30–45 minutes. Patch test first.
